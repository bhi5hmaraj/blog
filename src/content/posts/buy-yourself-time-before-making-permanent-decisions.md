title: "Buy yourself time before making permanent decisions"
description: "Temporary choices can preserve the option to make a better permanent one."
date: 2024-08-30

---

Urgency makes the first acceptable option look unusually valuable. This is especially true when moving to a new city, looking for a home, or making another decision under logistical pressure.

A temporary base can be worth paying for because it buys time. It reduces the fear that someone else will take the option, makes it easier to inspect alternatives, and prevents a token payment from turning into a larger commitment.

The local question is usually, "How do I make this option work?" The more useful question may be, "Can I solve the larger problem somewhere else?" Preserving options is not indecision when the cost of reversing a rushed decision is high.
