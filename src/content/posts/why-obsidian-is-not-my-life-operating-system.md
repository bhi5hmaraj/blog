title: "Why Obsidian is not my life operating system"
description: "Life critical information needs less categorisation and more reliability."
date: 2024-11-14
tags: [personal-systems, tools, obsidian, ai-assisted]
draft: true
---

Obsidian is useful, but I do not want it to become the control panel for everything important in my life. Health, finances, mood, and decisions need a system that still works when I am tired and least interested in maintaining it.

Choosing the correct file creates more friction than it seems. If capture requires categorisation, some thoughts will never be recorded. If retrieval depends on remembering my old taxonomy, important information can exist without being available.

For life critical information, my requirements are boring: quick capture, reliable sync, easy review, and confidence that I can recover it. Everything else is secondary.
