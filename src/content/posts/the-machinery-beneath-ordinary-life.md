I am starting to appreciate the simple things I normally take for granted.

Health is not permanent. The body is a complicated machine doing an enormous amount of work just to make an ordinary day feel ordinary. When it works, it disappears from attention. You notice the machinery only after some part of it stops being quiet.

There is something useful in remembering this before a failure forces the lesson. Flourishing is not the default background of life. It is something worth noticing while it is happening.
