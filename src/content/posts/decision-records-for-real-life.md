When I make a consequential decision, I now want to leave a note for my future self. It should contain the options I considered, their costs, the assumptions I made, and what could go wrong.

Memory compresses the reasoning and preserves the outcome. If the decision goes badly, hindsight makes the mistake look obvious. If it goes well, luck gets mistaken for judgment. A short decision record preserves what was actually visible at the time.

The useful question is not only which option looks best. It is whether I am comparing the best case of one option with the worst case of another. I should also ask what information would change my mind and whether I can preserve more options before committing.
