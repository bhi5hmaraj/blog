Sometimes it is difficult to notice that you are going through something. You may still appear functional while enjoyment fades, small tasks become irritating, and the future stops feeling concrete.

The gradual change is what makes it dangerous. Memory gets worse, work becomes harder, and basic adult tasks start slipping. Each failure produces guilt, which is then treated as evidence of a character problem rather than another sign that something is wrong.
