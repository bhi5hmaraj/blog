I started with pieces that were familiar enough to keep pulling me back to the keyboard. The progress was not dramatic. I learnt the opening of one piece, improved at reading sheet music, and then moved a little further.

That is probably the right scale for learning an instrument. A song is too large to be the unit of progress. A section, a transition, or a few bars can be finished and repeated without needing a heroic practice session.
