I have never used Instagram and left Facebook years ago. I still think that was mostly good for my attention and agency. But opting out has costs that are easy to ignore.

Social networks are also practical infrastructure. People use them to find homes, events, communities, collaborators, and help from acquaintances. Refusing the attention market can also mean giving up access to weak ties that carry useful information.

I do not think the answer is a blanket claim that social media is bad. Going cold turkey without understanding the role it plays can replace one problem with another. The better question is which functions I want, which incentives I reject, and whether I can recover the useful parts without surrendering the rest.
