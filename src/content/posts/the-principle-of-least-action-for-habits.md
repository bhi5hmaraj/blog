The first requirement for a personal system is not elegance. It is whether I have enough motivation and willpower to sustain it.

This is a sort of principle of least action for habits. A routine that depends on the best version of me is not a routine. It is an occasional performance. The useful design question is how little activation energy the action can require while still moving me in the right direction.

The smallest version should work on an ordinary bad day. Everything beyond that is optional.
