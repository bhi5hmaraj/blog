---
title: "The best system is one you can trust"
description: "Tools matter less than whether the system survives ordinary life."
date: 2024-11-14
tags: [personal-systems, tools, ai-assisted]
draft: true
---

I used to spend a lot of time looking for the right tool. Over time I have realised that it mostly does not matter. If a tool is stable, easy to reach, and helps me find what I wrote, it is probably enough. Sometimes a notebook is enough.

Tool hopping is often a symptom of a different problem. When I am absorbed in the goal, I use whatever is available. When I am avoiding the goal, improving the system feels like progress.

The most important feature is trust. I need to trust that my thoughts are not disappearing, that everything is synced, and that I can find something when it matters. A system with fewer features that I actually use is better than a beautiful system I am always repairing.
