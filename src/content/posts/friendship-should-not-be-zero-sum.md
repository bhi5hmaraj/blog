An important friendship should not require every other relationship to become less important. Yet attention is limited, boundaries are ambiguous, and privacy can look like secrecy from the outside.

I want a better language for handling this. Honesty does not require sharing everything. Privacy does not remove the need to communicate decisions that affect someone else. Neglect can be real without turning every relationship into a competition for priority.
