Not having enough bandwidth to do ordinary things is itself information. A missed message or delayed chore looks isolated, but the pattern can spread across work, money, health, and relationships.

I used to treat each failure as a separate problem. I needed more discipline here, a better reminder there, and another system somewhere else. But if several unrelated parts of life are deteriorating together, the upstream constraint may be attention, health, or stress.

This changes the response. Adding another productivity system will not restore missing capacity. The first step is to notice the pattern and reduce the load before making more commitments.
