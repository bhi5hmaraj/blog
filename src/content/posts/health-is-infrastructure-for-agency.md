Health is easy to treat as one concern among many when it is going well. But it is upstream of almost everything else. Memory, judgment, motivation, and the ability to imagine a future all depend on it.

Changes can also be gradual. You adapt to each small decline and keep explaining individual mistakes as laziness or carelessness. Only later do you notice that the person making decisions had less capacity than before.

This has made me more careful about moralising failures of attention and energy, both in myself and in other people. Agency is not detached from the body. The machinery doing the choosing needs maintenance too.
